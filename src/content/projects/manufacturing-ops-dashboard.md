## Overview

The dashboard was designed to present operational data without the complexity of a heavy application shell. A static-first architecture kept deployment simple and made the interface resilient for distributed teams.

## What I handled

- Information architecture for production and maintenance reporting
- Component design system for repeated KPI modules
- Cloudflare-friendly static deployment workflow

## Result

The shipped interface gave supervisors a faster way to review throughput and downtime trends, while the content-first structure made updates straightforward for non-engineering contributors.
