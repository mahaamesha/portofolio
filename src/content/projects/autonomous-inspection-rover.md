## Overview

This project explored a compact rover platform for low-risk visual inspection in hard-to-access industrial environments. The challenge was to create a housing strategy that balanced sensor visibility, service access, and fabrication simplicity.

## What I handled

- CAD concept modeling for the outer shell and internal component placement
- Review-ready visualization for stakeholder sign-off
- Design adjustments based on thermal and maintenance constraints

## Result

The final prototype reduced assembly complexity while keeping serviceable access points for the battery, compute module, and sensor package. The model package also became the baseline for subsequent electronics integration.
