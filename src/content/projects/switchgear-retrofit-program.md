## Overview

This program centered on modernizing switchgear documentation and improving the reliability of handoff materials used during maintenance preparation. The work had to stay precise, readable, and aligned with the realities of field execution.

## What I handled

- Structured documentation for field technicians and reviewers
- Coordination of panel layout considerations and maintenance access
- Test preparation support and issue tracking during execution

## Result

The retrofit package improved clarity for both maintenance crews and decision-makers, reducing rework and helping the team move through preparation with greater confidence.
